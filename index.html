Core capabilities

Everything a digital estate plan needs, in one place.

Built around the problems families run into most: scattered accounts, forgotten passwords, and no instructions left behind.

Centralized digital vault

Catalog social media, email, cloud storage, financial accounts, documents and crypto wallets in one encrypted place instead of scattered across platforms.

Trusted beneficiaries

Assign nominees per asset, set their trust level, and control exactly who is authorized to receive what — nobody sees more than you allow.

Digital will & legacy messages

Write instructions and schedule messages that stay locked until a condition you define — inactivity, verified death certificate, or manual release.

Strong authentication

Encrypted credentials, two-factor authentication and secure recovery reduce the risk of unauthorized access to what matters most.

Role-based access control

Beneficiaries, executors and admins each see only what their role permits — access is granted by design, not by accident.

Full audit logging

Every login, edit and release attempt is time-stamped and recorded, so activity on the vault is always traceable.

How it works

From scattered accounts to a plan that executes itself.

01

User organizes assets

Add and categorize every digital asset in the encrypted vault.

02

Vault secures & stores

Assets are encrypted with access rules and release conditions attached.

03

Trigger condition is met

Inactivity period elapses, or a verified event/manual release occurs.

04

Beneficiary gets access

Only the assigned, authorized beneficiary receives the asset or message.
